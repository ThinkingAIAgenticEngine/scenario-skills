# MMORPG Genre Template

## Genre Characteristics

- Immersive experience, role-playing
- Dungeon combat as core
- Strong social interaction, guild system
- Multi-dimensional progression, long-term growth

## Progression System List

### Core Progression

| Progression System | Description | Associated Gift Pack Types |
|--------------------|-------------|----------------------------|
| Equipment | Power core | Equipment enhancement, Equipment upgrade, Sets, Equipment refine |
| Skill | Class core | Skill books, Skill upgrade, Skill points |
| Pet/Companion | Companion progression | Pet eggs, Pet upgrade, Pet skill books |
| Mount | Movement/Attribute bonus | Mount advancement, Mount cultivation materials, Mount equipment |

### Advanced Progression

## Gameplay Classification

### PVE Gameplay

| Gameplay | Description | Output/Associated Gift Pack | Trigger Scenario |
|----------|-------------|------------------------------|------------------|
| Main Dungeon | Story stages | Equipment materials, Experience potions, Pet fragments | Stage failure, Stage three-star, Chapter completion |
| Elite Dungeon | High difficulty stages | Elite materials, Equipment fragments, Rare items | Challenge failure, Rewards not fully claimed |
| Abyss/Tower | Extreme challenge | Tower coins, Rare materials, Equipment fragments | Floor stuck, Challenge failure, Breakthrough floor |
| World Boss | Server-wide challenge | Boss rewards, Rare materials, Equipment fragments | Low damage ranking, Killed, Participation reward |
| Team Dungeon | Multi-player collaboration | Equipment materials, Pet fragments, Mount materials | Dungeon failure, Team disbanded, Rewards insufficient |
| Secret Realm/Dungeon | Random explore | Secret realm materials, Gems, Rare items | Exploration failure, Chest open, Floor complete |
| Daily Dungeon | Resource acquisition | Daily materials, Experience potions, Gold | Stamina shortage, Attempts exhausted |

### PVP Gameplay

| Gameplay | Description | Output/Associated Gift Pack | Trigger Scenario |
|----------|-------------|------------------------------|------------------|
| Arena | 1v1 battle | Arena coins, Honor points, Equipment materials | Rank drop, Challenge failure, Win streak broken |
| Guild War/Clan War | Team competition | Guild contribution, Guild coins, Rare materials | Battle failure, Contribution low, City lost |
| Territory War/Castle Siege | Clan contest | Territory rewards, Rare materials, Equipment fragments | City lost, War declaration success/failure |
| Cross-server War | Server competition | Cross-server rewards, Rare materials, Titles | Battle failure, Rank drop |
| 3v3/5v5 Arena | Team competition | Arena coins, Honor points, Equipment materials | Match failure, Tier drop |
| Wild PK | Free battle | Equipment materials, Revenge gift pack | Killed, Equipment dropped |
